## 1. Reference analysis (what I found)

**On your Realme RMX5313** (Android 16, Unisoc ums9230 / Mali‑G57 MP1, 720×1600 @ 90 Hz, 6 GB RAM). This is a low‑to‑mid GPU, so it's our performance target: if the game holds 60 fps here, it will run almost anywhere.

Reference app (v2.2.0, Unity, AppLovin ads). Screens and mechanics I captured:

| Area | What the reference does |
|---|---|
| Home | Logo over a live 3D scene, "Level N", Setting + Skin buttons, name text field, rewarded "+10 size" button, "Tap to Start". No bottom bar, no real meta features. |

There's a ski pickup for speed, a "STAGE 1" label, and a podium at the top. |
| Ads | Banner during gameplay, plus interstitials **during and between stages** (I hit two long playable ads in one run). |

**Play Store:** 3.1★ from 42.5K reviews, 50M+ downloads, rated 3+. The three most‑liked reviews all say the same things:
1. Ads interrupt the race mid‑run ("10 ads in 1 stage").
2. Coins don't seem to add up, so skins feel unreachable.
3. "Snow gets spent too fast": players can't tell whether their ball is big enough.

**What we take from this:** the genre loop (roll, grow, bridge, race) is proven at 50M+ installs, but the execution leaves a lot of room. We win by fixing all three complaints and adding real depth and polish.

## 2. Originality and Play Policy guardrails

We build an **original game in the same genre**: no copied name, logo, characters, art, colours, UI layout, sounds or level layouts.

| Element | Reference | Ours (original) |
|---|---|---|
| Name and logo | "Snow Race!!" in cyan and orange | New name, own logo (icy indigo, aurora mint and berry pink), never uses "Snow Race" as the title |
| Characters | Solid‑colour stickmen | **"Snowpals"**: chubby bean‑shaped mascots with beanies, scarves, mittens and big eyes, plus animal kits (penguin, polar cub, fox, reindeer, yeti…) |
| Bridges | Fixed coloured rail lane per racer | **Free‑form shared bridges**: drop your ball anywhere along the shore, the bridge extends in the direction you pushed, and anyone can use any bridge |
| Finish | Podium | **Snowman Finale**: your leftover ball becomes the base of a giant snowman, and its size sets a ×1 to ×5 coin multiplier |
| Feedback | None | **Bridge‑Ready meter**: shows exactly whether your ball can span the next gap |
| Ads | Mid‑race interstitials, banner | **Never mid‑race.** Rewarded ads only at natural breaks, and only once you integrate an ad SDK |

Policy checklist (built in from day one):
- No trademarked names or assets. Every graphic, sound and level is generated by our own code and tools.
- Cartoon "bonk" only: no skulls, "KO" or "Kill" wording. Fits an **Everyone** rating.
- No permissions besides `VIBRATE`, and no `INTERNET` until ads are added. The privacy policy matches that exactly (offline, local save only).
- The fake "watch ad" flow exists **only in debug builds**. Release builds never show a simulated ad.
- `targetSdk 37` covers edge‑to‑edge insets and predictive back. The manifest declares `android:appCategory="game"` so the portrait lock still applies on large screens under Android 16+.
- Pure Kotlin/GL with no native `.so` files, so we're 16 KB page‑size compliant automatically.
- Target audience: I'll recommend declaring **13+**. If you want under‑13 users later, any ad SDK must be Families‑certified (I'll note this in code and docs).

## 3. Game design

### 3.1 Core loop (one race, 45 to 120 s)
1. **Intro flyover** (2 s), then a 3‑2‑1‑GO countdown.
2. **Hold and drag anywhere** to steer (floating joystick, with tuned dead‑zone and smoothing). Release and your Snowpal stops.
3. **Roll to grow.** Fresh snow adds volume and leaves a carved groove with raised berms. You can't regrow on carved snow, so where you roll matters. A bigger ball is slightly slower: a speed‑vs‑size trade‑off.
4. **Bridge the gap.** Push your ball into the water edge and it packs into bridge blocks that extend as the ball shrinks. Run out and the bridge stops, so go back for more snow. Bridges are shared, and blocks carry your colour flag.
5. **Bonk rivals.** If your ball is ≥15 % bigger (or theirs is gone), you bonk them: they tumble with dizzy stars, lose 60 % of their snow and are stunned for 1 s. Shields block bonks.
6. **Stages.** Each level has 1 to 4 stages (snowfield + gap), then a final ramp.
7. **Snowman Finale.** Your leftover ball sets the coin multiplier (×1 to ×5) on a step staircase. Rank and multiplier pay out.
8. **Fall in the water**: splash, then respawn on the last shore after 1.5 s with your ball reset.

### 3.2 My additions (beyond the reference)
- **Free‑form shared bridges**, **Bridge‑Ready meter** and **Snowman Finale** (see above).
- **4 power‑ups:** Sled Boost (speed), Frost Shield (bonk immunity), Snow Magnet (2× growth), Mega Mitten (+40 % size instantly).
- **8 hazards**, introduced gradually: ice patches (slippery), rolling logs, sideways snow‑plow, penguin parade crossing, cracking thin ice, wind‑gust zones, bouncy candy mushrooms, steam vents that melt your ball.
- **8 biomes × 3 times of day:** Alpine Village, Frozen Lake, Pine Valley, Candy Peaks, Ice Caves, Aurora Tundra, Glacier Harbor, Steam Springs. Each comes in day, sunset and aurora‑night variants, with weather (light snow, blizzard, clear).
- **Boss levels every 10th level**, alternating: *Avalanche Escape* (an avalanche chases you uphill) and *Mega Ball* (a giant rolling ball sweeps the field).
- **Meta systems:** 7‑day login calendar, a daily Lucky Wheel, 3 daily missions, 30 achievements, a milestone chest every 10 levels, and a stats profile.
- **Collection:** 24 Snowpals, 20 ball skins (classic, candy‑cane, donut, planet, disco…) and 12 trail effects (sparkle, rainbow, hearts…), each in a rarity tier.
- **Pre‑race boosters:** Big Start, Turbo Start and Shield Start, bought with coins (or with a debug mock ad).
- **Fair‑play pledge:** no ad ever interrupts a race. This directly fixes the #1 complaint.

### 3.3 Economy (fixes complaint #2)
- Coins per race: 1st place 60, 2nd 40, 3rd 25, 4th or lower 10. Then × Finale multiplier (1 to 5). Gift‑box pickups add 5 to 15 each.
- Gems come from the login calendar, missions, achievements and milestone chests (never purchasable yet).
- Prices: common Snowpal 600, rare 1,800, epic 4,000, legendary 60 gems. An average player unlocks something every 3 to 5 levels.
- Every coin gain is animated (coins fly to the counter with sound), so players **see** the reward.

### 3.4 Progression and unlock rules (as you asked)
- Fresh install (debug **and** release): **only Level 1 is unlocked.**
- Clearing a level (top 3) unlocks the next one. Stars: 1st = ★★★, 2nd = ★★, 3rd = ★. 4th or lower means retry.
- **Debug build:** tapping any *locked* level opens a **"Watch Ad to Unlock"** dialog. Tapping *Watch Ad* plays a mock full‑screen ad (labelled "DEBUG AD", 3 s countdown) and then unlocks **that** level.
- **Release build:** tapping a locked level shows *"Clear Level N first"* with a button that goes to Level N. A single flag (`AdConfig.rewardedUnlockEnabled`) switches release to the ad flow once you plug in a real ad SDK. The `AdProvider` interface is ready for AdMob, AppLovin or others.

### 3.5 5,000 levels, and how they stay "perfect"
- **Levels 1 to 10 are handcrafted** (JSON in `assets/levels/`) and tuned so **the player wins**:

| # | Name | Introduces | Stages | Rivals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First Roll | Hold and drag, grow, bridge (hand‑pointer tutorial) | 1 | 3 |
| 2 | Two Shores | Bridge‑Ready meter | 2 | 3 |
| 3 | Bonk! | Bigger ball bonks rivals (scripted tiny rival) | 2 | 3 |
| 4 | Gift Hunt | Gift‑box coins | 2 | 4 |
| 5 | Sled Boost | First power‑up | 2 | 4 |
| 6 | Slippery | Ice patches | 2 | 4 |
| 7 | Log Roll | Rolling logs (slow, clearly telegraphed) | 2 | 4 |
| 8 | Shield Up | Frost Shield | 2 | 4 |
| 9 | Three Peaks | 3 stages | 3 | 4 |
| 10 | Avalanche! | First boss (gentle) → celebration, chest and a new Snowpal | 2 | 4 |

  **Win guarantee for 1 to 10:** a *Race Director* rubber‑bands rivals so they stay behind the player's progress. Rivals never target the player, grow more slowly, and are capped at 85 % of player speed. If the player falls in, they respawn instantly and keep their ball. As long as you play, you finish 1st.
- **Levels 11 to 5,000** are generated **deterministically from a seed** (no 5,000 data files, so the APK stays tiny). The difficulty curve rises smoothly with a saw‑tooth: every 10‑level block ramps, peaks at the boss, then gives a relief level. It plateaus around level 400 so it never becomes impossible. The biome changes every 25 levels, and stage count, gap width, rival count (4 to 7), AI skill, hazards and power‑ups all scale with the curve.
- **Validation guarantees.** Every level must pass before it ships; if it fails, the generator re‑rolls deterministically with a salted seed.
  1. Same seed gives the same level (hash check).
  2. Reachable snow on each stage is ≥ 1.8× the bridge volume the gap needs (BFS over walkable cells).
  3. There's a clear path from spawn to every gap and on to the finish.
  4. No hazard sits within 3 m of a spawn or landing point, moving hazards always leave ≥ 1.2 s safe windows, and props never overlap.
  5. A **headless autopilot** (skill 0.85) finishes top‑3 on every level, and a skill‑0.5 autopilot wins levels 1 to 10. Estimated race time is 40 to 150 s.
  - A JUnit suite checks all **5,000** levels (full run in WP12), plus a fast 300‑level sample for everyday test runs.

## 5. Graphics: the playground gets the most time

No downloaded 3D models. **Everything is generated in code** (meshes, textures, shaders), so it's 100 % original and the APK stays small. Look: *bright, clean, stylised low‑poly with soft lighting*.

**Engine (custom, OpenGL ES 3.0):**
- Toon‑lit shading: 3‑band ramp, warm sun, cool blue ambient shadows on snow, rim light.
- **Shadow mapping:** 1024² with PCF on High, 512² on Medium, blob shadows on Low.
- MSAA 4× (almost free on Mali tile GPUs), per‑biome colour‑grading LUT, vignette, optional bloom on High.
- **Auto quality tier** from GPU detection, plus **dynamic resolution** (0.7 to 1.0) driven by frame time. Frame rate locked to a stable 60 fps.

**Playground:**
- **Deformable snow:** each stage has a heightmap texture the ball "paints" into. The vertex shader carves a groove with raised berms, and normals come from the heightmap. The same grid drives gameplay (how much snow is left).
- **Snow material:** subsurface‑blue tint, view‑dependent sparkle glints, soft berm shadows.
- **Water:** animated normal noise, fresnel sky reflection, shoreline and pillar foam, floating ice chunks that bob, and splash rings.
- **Bridges:** packed‑snow blocks drop in with squash and a dust puff, flags in the owner's colour, and rising pitch per block.
- **World:** instanced snowy pines with wind sway, cabins, waving snowman spectators, bunting and flags (vertex animation), layered distant mountains with atmospheric fog, cloud billboards, GPU snowfall, sun glow, and an ice‑castle finish arch with balloons, confetti cannons and fireworks at night.

**Characters and animation (procedural rig):**
- Snowpals built from primitives with an inverted‑hull outline. Animation covers run cycle, push pose, idle breathing, eye blinks, a scarf on a verlet‑physics chain, bonk tumble with dizzy stars, splash fall, a victory dance and squash‑and‑stretch landings.
- **Ball:** noise‑displaced clumpy icosphere, rotation that matches travel, spray particles, and a contact shadow that grows with it.

**Camera and FX:**
- Spring‑damped follow cam with look‑ahead that zooms out as the ball grows. FOV kick on boost, impact screen shake, a 0.4× slow‑mo moment when you take 1st, and an orbit cam at the finish.
- Particles: snow puffs, impact rings, speed lines, coin sparkles, confetti.
- Name tags rendered once to a texture atlas, then drawn as GPU billboards (cheap).

**App icon and store art:** a Python/PIL renderer (supersampled) draws the Snowpal mascot leaping over a glossy giant snowball on an aurora sky. Outputs: adaptive icon (foreground, background and **monochrome** layers) for every density, a 512² Play icon and a 1024×500 feature graphic.

## 6. Sound

All sounds are **synthesised by our own script** (`tools/gen_audio.py`), so there's zero licence risk. Music is encoded to AAC with macOS `afconvert`.
- **SFX (~30):** UI tap, back, panel open, coin, coin‑tick, star 1/2/3, unlock jingle, chest open, wheel tick, countdown and GO, snow crunch footsteps, **rolling‑snow loop** (pitch follows speed, volume follows ball size), bridge‑block thud (rising pitch), splash, bonk, boost whoosh, shield hum, power‑up pickup, avalanche rumble, win fanfare, lose sting, error buzz.
- **Music:** a cosy winter menu theme (bells, plucks, soft pad, ~40 s seamless loop), an upbeat race theme (~140 bpm loop), and a boss‑level variant. Crossfades between scenes, and music ducks under fanfares.
- **Haptics:** `VibrationEffect` primitives (tick, click, thud) on API 30+, with fallback for older versions. Respects the setting.
- Honest note: synthesised audio is clean and polished but not studio‑orchestral. The audio layer is data‑driven, so you can drop in licensed tracks later with no code change.

## 7. Technical architecture

- **Stack:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose (all menus and HUD), `GLSurfaceView` + OpenGL ES 3.0 (gameplay, Home diorama, Shop preview, onboarding scenes). Fixed 60 Hz simulation with render interpolation.
- **Dependencies added:** `core-splashscreen`, `navigation-compose`, `lifecycle-viewmodel-compose`, `datastore-preferences`, `kotlinx-serialization-json` (+ plugin). No game engine, no ad SDK yet, no internet.
- **Persistence:** DataStore for settings, economy and profile. A compact binary file stores stars for all 5,000 levels, written atomically.
- **Ads abstraction:** `AdProvider` (`showRewarded`, `showInterstitial`). `MockAdProvider` is used in debug only and `NoAdProvider` in release, both behind `AdConfig`.
- **Package layout:**
```
com.ngstudio.snowrace
├─ app/          App, AppContainer (manual DI), MainActivity, navigation
├─ core/         math (Vec3/Mat4/Quat), rng, time, easing

├─ audio/        AudioEngine (SoundPool + music), SoundBank, Haptics
├─ ads/          AdProvider, MockAdProvider, NoAdProvider, AdConfig
├─ level/        LevelSpec, Handcrafted (1–10), LevelGenerator, DifficultyCurve, Biomes, LevelValidator
├─ game/
│  ├─ sim/       World, Racer, Snowball, SnowField, Bridge, Hazards, PowerUps, Physics, RaceDirector, AiBrain, Autopilot
│  ├─ render/    GameRenderer, Shader programs, MeshBuilder, Camera, ShadowMap, Particles, CharacterRig, TextureGen, PostFx, QualityTier
│  ├─ input/     FloatingJoystick
│  └─ GameScreen, GameViewModel, HUD
└─ ui/           theme, components (GameButton, Panel, Ribbon, CoinCounter, BottomBar, Snowfall, Dialogs),
                 splash, onboarding, home, levels, shop, rewards, profile, settings, legal, result
assets/  legal/{terms,privacy}.html + legal.css + font,  levels/handcrafted.json
res/     font/, raw/ (sfx, music), mipmap-* (icons)
tools/   gen_icon.py, gen_audio.py, gen_store_art.py
```
- **Build:** debug gets the debug menu and mock ads. Release has R8 on (already enabled in your Gradle file), no debug UI and no mock ads. You add the signing config with your keystore later.

## 11. Honest limits

- No 3D artist assets: the look is high‑polish **stylised procedural** (think clean low‑poly with good lighting), not hand‑sculpted models. The renderer accepts mesh data, so artist models can be swapped in later.
- "5,000 perfect levels" means 10 hand‑tuned levels plus 4,990 generated levels that each pass automated solvability, fairness and autopilot checks. They won't each be individually hand‑designed.
- This is a large build (roughly 15–20K lines). It runs as one continuous phase, but it will take many hours of work in this session, with device checks along the way.
