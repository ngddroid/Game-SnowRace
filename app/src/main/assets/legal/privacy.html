1 What’s saved on your device

To work, the Game saves a small amount of information in its own storage on your device:

  • Game progressSuch as the levels you’ve completed and the stars you’ve earned.
  • Virtual itemsYour virtual coins and gems, and the cosmetic items you’ve unlocked.
  • SettingsYour choices for options such as sound, vibration, graphics, notifications and data sharing.
  • Nickname (optional)The name you type in, shown only on your device next to your character.

This information stays on your device. We can’t see it, and we never receive a copy.

2 What the Game sends, and why

While “Share usage & crash data” is on (it is on by default), the Game sends the following to Google Firebase, which processes it for us:

  • Game statistics (Firebase Analytics)Events such as starting or finishing a level and your result (place, stars, multiplier, coins), items you unlock with virtual coins or gems, rewards you collect and screens you open. Firebase adds device information (model, Android version, language), the app version, an approximate country or region worked out from your IP address, and a random ID that Firebase creates for this installation. We use it to see which levels are too hard, what players enjoy and what to improve.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)If the Game crashes: the technical details of the error, the device model, Android version, graphics chip, available memory, the level and screen you were on, and a random installation ID. We use it only to find and fix bugs.
  • Notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)A random token that lets us deliver push notifications about new levels, events and updates. It exists only while notifications are on, and is deleted when you switch them off.
  • Update check (Google Play)The Game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can offer you the update. Google Play handles this under Google’s privacy policy.

None of this includes your name, nickname, email address, contacts or advertising ID. We don’t use it to identify you, we don’t use it for advertising, and we never sell it. The Game does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • collect your real name, email address, phone number or contacts;
  • access your precise location, camera, microphone, photos or files;
  • use your advertising ID, or track you across other apps and websites;
  • show ads or share data with advertising networks.

If you email us

If you choose to contact us by email, we’ll receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and help you, we don’t share it or use it for marketing, and we delete it once it’s no longer needed for that.

3 Permissions

The Game uses these device permissions:

  • Vibration VIBRATEHaptic feedback while you play. Switch it off in Settings at any time.
  • Internet INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATETo send the statistics and crash reports above, receive push notifications and check for updates. You can play the Game without a connection.
  • Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONSOn Android 13 and newer, the Game asks before showing notifications: reminders when your daily gift or free spin is ready (planned on your device) and news from us. If you say no, the Game works exactly the same.

The Game doesn’t ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

4 Third parties

We use these services from Google:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging) processes the data in section 2 on our behalf, as our service provider. See Firebase privacy and security and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play provides the in-app update check.

We don’t share data with anyone else, and the Game contains no advertising or social-media SDKs. Firebase may process data on servers outside your country, protected by Google’s data-transfer safeguards.

About app stores

The app store you got the Game from (for example, Google Play) handles your download under its own privacy policy, not this one. Stores may also give developers install statistics and, if you’ve allowed your device to share diagnostic data, crash and performance reports.

5 Keeping and deleting your data

You’re in control:

  • Stop sending. Switch off “Share usage & crash data” in Settings and the Game stops sending statistics and crash reports straight away. Crash reports waiting on your device are deleted, not sent.
  • Stop notifications. Switch off “Notifications” in Settings, or in your Android settings.
  • Reset progress. “Reset progress” in Settings deletes all of the Game’s data on your device (progress, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings and nickname) and gives this installation a new random analytics ID.
  • Uninstall. Uninstalling the Game deletes all of its data from your device.
  • Device backup. If Android backup is on, Android may include the Game’s data in your device backup (for example, in your Google account) so it can be restored later. That backup is handled by Android and your backup provider, and we can’t access it.

Firebase keeps game statistics for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, then deletes them automatically. You can also ask us to delete them by email (see section 10).

6 Security

Your game data is kept in the Game’s own app storage on your device, which Android keeps separate from other apps. Statistics, crash reports and notifications travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and are stored by Google Firebase under Google’s security practices.

Protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping its software up to date helps keep your game data safe too.

7 Children’s privacy

The optional nickname stays on the device. We suggest that kids pick a fun nickname rather than their real name. Parents can switch off “Share usage & crash data” and “Notifications” in Settings.
