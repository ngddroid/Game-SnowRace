Roll, grow and race to the top in Frosty Roll Rally, the snowy 3D racing game where your snowball is your ride and your bridge!

Push your snowball over fresh snow to make it bigger. When you reach icy water, your snow builds an ice bridge so you can cross before your rivals. Every snow race is a choice: grab more snow, or rush ahead?

❄ HOW TO PLAY
• Drag one finger to steer and roll your snowball over fresh snow
• Reach the water and build an ice bridge with the snow you collected
• Crash into smaller rivals with a bigger snowball to knock them off their feet
• Climb the rainbow finish stairs: the higher you get, the bigger your reward, up to x5!

❄ 5,000 LEVELS IN 8 WINTER WORLDS
Race through Alpine Village, Frozen Lake, Pine Valley, Candy Peaks, Ice Caves, Aurora Tundra, Glacier Harbor and Steam Springs. Each world brings new twists: thin ice, gusts of wind, rolling logs, snowplows, bumpers, steam vents and waddling penguins. Then face the boss races against the Avalanche and the Mega Ball!
